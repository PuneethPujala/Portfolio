My story

Building things
that matter.

I'm a computer science student and founder based at VIT-AP University, Andhra Pradesh. I build AI-powered systems that solve real problems — not demos, but products that actually ship.

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, full-stack engineering, and product thinking. I've trained RL agents from scratch, built voice assistants that run entirely on-device, and architected mobile health platforms for elderly users who can't afford cloud dependency.

I believe the next generation of AI should work for people without internet, on mid-range Android phones, in rural India — not just in Silicon Valley data centers. That belief is what CareMyMed is built on.

Pacman-using-RL / 2025

Reinforcement Learning,
debugged from first principles

Didn't just run an RL framework — diagnosed three fundamental algorithmic bugs in Approximate Q-Learning and fixed them: a bootstrap miscalculation making the agent blind to future rewards, static hyperparameters causing weight oscillation, and a "disappearing feature weight" bug punishing the agent for eating food. Added a custom corridor-danger feature that learned a −971 weight for ghost traps.
